**Study:** Handley and Molloy (2022), *SME corporate governance: a literature review of informal mechanisms for governance*, Meditari Accountancy Research [1].

## The question

How does governance work in small and medium enterprises (SMEs), which often have no formal board of directors, or only a board in name [1]?

## Method and sample

A structured literature review of articles indexed in Scopus, using bibliometric software to map the field. The authors applied two search methods, which returned 455 and 237 articles [1]. The authors are based in Australia.

## Key findings

- **No theory built for SMEs.** Research on SME governance mostly borrows theories built for large listed companies, especially agency theory. These fit owner-managed firms poorly, because ownership and control are often in the same hands [1].
- **Informal mechanisms do the work.** SMEs reach the skills and oversight that a board would provide through other routes: advisory or outsourced boards, business networks, capital providers, accountants and lawyers [1].
- **The main conflict shifts.** In family or closely held firms, the central agency problem is less between owners and managers and more between dominant and minority owners. Close personal ties can weaken objectivity [1].
- **Gaps in the evidence.** Research is mostly quantitative and European. Emerging economies are under-studied [1].

## Limitations

The authors narrowed the article set using citation frequency, so recent or emerging lines of research may be under-represented [1]. As a literature review, it summarizes existing studies and does not test new data.

## What it means for practitioners

- An owner-managed business can build proportionate governance without a formal board. An advisory board, a trusted outside accountant and lender reporting are legitimate governance mechanisms.
- Where there are several owners, written rules that protect minority owners deserve priority.
- This is consistent with ISO 37000:2021, which presents governance as applicable to organizations of every size and type [2].
