# LIV — Fee structure (proposal)

Status: **prices approved by the owner on October 5, 2026** (still subject to the adviser checks in §10 before publication; `PUBLIC_FEES` stays off until the owner decides to publish). All amounts are in **US dollars** and are **starting points to test with the first partners**, not market quotes: no competitor's prices were checked. Before publishing, review with your US agent's lawyers and a tax adviser (see §10). The system's price catalog (Admin → Billing → Price catalog) is seeded with these numbers and every price can be changed there without code; old prices keep their history.

## 1. Principles
1. **Fees pay for work, never for outcomes.** No fee depends on a decision being favorable. The application fee is charged whether the decision is yes or no; exam fees are the same for everyone; nobody (agent, partner, trainer) is paid by results.
2. **Public and uniform.** One public fee schedule; discounts are published categories, not private deals. Every discount is recorded on the partner's account with its reason.
3. **Impartiality over cash.** Non-payment can pause *services* (issuing new certificates), but it never changes an accreditation finding. The two are separate in the agreement and in the system ("service hold" vs "accreditation status").
4. **Predictable for partners.** A yearly fee that includes a certificate allowance, then a simple per-certificate rate that falls with volume.
5. **Affordable entry for the GCC/MENA market.** A Starter tier and a founding-partner program; optional regional price bands for candidate fees (§6).
6. **No consulting for accreditation.** LIV must not sell partners help in obtaining accreditation: that is a conflict of interest for an accreditation body and is a point assessors check. The consulting idea in the original vision is therefore limited to generic public training and content, or placed in a separate entity with a documented firewall (confirm with counsel and the accreditation consultant).

## 6. Regional price bands (optional)
To keep the candidate exam affordable across MENA, apply a band by **billing country** (not by IP):
- **Band A** (Gulf states and high-income markets): list price.
- **Band B** (lower-income MENA markets): about **65%** of list for candidate exam fees only. Partner fees stay uniform to keep accreditation fees impartial and simple. Review bands yearly and publish the country list.

## 9. Payment terms
- **Currency:** USD. Display of approximate local currency allowed; charge in USD.
- **Methods:** card (Stripe, when enabled), bank transfer (ACH, wire or SWIFT; the payer bears bank charges), other local methods later. Invoices for the first partners are issued manually and paid by bank transfer.
- **When:** application fee before assessment starts; annual fee in advance on the plan anniversary; certificate usage **monthly in arrears** (invoices under $50 roll forward); other services on delivery. Invoices are due in **14 days**.
- **Taxes:** fees are exclusive of VAT/GST and similar taxes. Where a tax applies, it is added to the invoice using the rate set for the partner (see §10) and the partner's tax number is shown.
- **Non-payment:** a reminder at due date; **service hold** (no new certificates) 30 days after the due date with written notice; an accreditation decision is never changed because of non-payment. Access to verify existing certificates always continues.
- **Refunds:** the application fee is not refundable once review begins; the annual fee is refunded pro rata only if LIV withdraws a service it can no longer provide; prepaid certificate bundles do not expire during the plan year and are refunded pro rata only if LIV ends the service; exam fees follow §5.
- **Disputes and chargebacks:** raised with LIV first (billing email); card chargebacks are answered with the invoice, agreement acceptance and usage records.

## 10. What to confirm with advisers
1. **Tax:** whether VAT/GST registration or collection is required in the Gulf states and MENA countries you target for digital services and for each fee, at what rate, and how B2B vs B2C is treated; set each partner's tax rate and label in Admin → Billing once confirmed (the system defaults to 0%).
2. **Sanctions and payment compliance:** screening of partners and payers before the first invoice.
3. **Agreement wording:** fees, service hold, refunds, disputes and governing law in the partner agreement.
4. **Bank details and legal address** for invoices (set in the environment, see `docs/BILLING.md`).
5. **Fee changes:** notice period for changes (suggest 60 days) in the agreement.

## 11. Illustration (numbers only to show how the fees combine)
| Partner | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter, 200 certificates a year, founding partner** | Application $250 + annual $450 + 100 certificates beyond the allowance × $4 = **$1,100** | Annual $450 (locked) + 100 × $4 = **$850** |
| **Starter, 200 certificates, standard** | $500 + $900 + $400 = **$1,800** | $900 + $400 = **$1,300** |
| **Professional, 1,200 certificates** | $1,000 + $2,400 + 700 × $4 = **$6,200** | $2,400 + $2,800 = **$5,200** |
| **Enterprise, 8,000 certificates** (annual quoted at $6,000) | $2,000 + $6,000 + (6,000 beyond the allowance: 1,000 × $4 + 4,000 × $3 + 1,000 × $2 = $18,000) = **$26,000** | $6,000 + $18,000 = **$24,000** |

Ten Starter and Professional partners at average first-year invoices of $2,000–$6,000 give roughly **$20,000–$60,000** in the first year before costs; exams, verification API and printing add to this later. Treat these as arithmetic, not forecasts.
