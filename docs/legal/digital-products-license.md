## 1. Scope and rights
This draft covers LIV resources only where LIV owns or has permission to license the content. A listing does not transfer copyright. DP01 must settle the exact personal/internal-business permissions, copying, adaptation and redistribution terms per product. Until that license is approved, a technical download is not a grant of unlimited commercial reuse.

## 2. Access classes
The current system supports free access with an account, access included in an active membership, and separately purchased access. A purchase records its price, resource version and access period. Access may be fixed-term or recorded without a time limit; the precise meaning of permanent access requires DP02. Membership expiry does not itself end a separate paid entitlement.

## 3. Restrictions
Do not remove attribution, falsely present content as an official professional award, share account credentials, or resell/distribute material beyond the approved license. Third-party material requires the applicable rights clearance; no ownership of a standard or external publication is implied.

## 4. Availability and revisions
Publication requires content review. The system can revise or retire resources, and currently blocks access when a resource is no longer published even if a purchase remains active. This is gap G07. DP02 must resolve continuity, replacement or remedies before an unqualified permanent-access promise is sold. Do not silently apply a new price or duration to an old order.

## 5. Preparation
Preparation is optional, uses material distinct from live examination content and never guarantees passing. Current sale rules require a linked operating program. No purchase creates eligibility or activates an unavailable assessment.

## 6. Support and remedies
Report missing access, infringement or material errors through support. The [Payment and Refund Policy](payment-and-refund-policy.md) applies when approved. Mandatory consumer remedies are not excluded.
