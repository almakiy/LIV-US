## 1. Charges
Only an identified approved fee schedule and described service may create a contractual charge. Currency is USD. P-6 remains open; proposed rates, included allowances, appeal fees and late-charge figures are not adopted here. Fees do not purchase authorization, promotion or favorable review.

## 2. Invoices
The system supports draft invoices, issue/freeze of billing details, numbered open invoices, paid invoices and voiding of unpaid invoices. Existing issued invoice amounts are not rewritten when catalog prices change. Review the service description, tax treatment and due date; report errors through billing support.

## 3. Payment
Stripe Checkout may accept the open balance when configured. Bank receipts are recorded manually. Partial bank receipts can reduce an invoice balance; the card flow requests the full open balance. Do not claim a payment has cleared merely because a receipt screenshot or return page exists.

## 4. Usage and adjustments
Usage billing accounts for recorded certificates and eligible allowances and seeks to avoid charging the same issuance twice. Approved discounts/credits need a category and reason. Neither billing volume nor a prepaid plan establishes quality or new scope rights. Disputes and refunds require the [Payment and Refund Policy](payment-and-refund-policy.md); voiding an unpaid invoice is not refunding a paid one.

## 5. Unpaid accounts
An authorized financial service hold can stop new issuance in portal/API channels. It does not change authorization status or disable verification of prior credentials. PB02 must settle notice, cure period, disputed invoices, lifting holds and any lawful late charge. No time period is invented from an example.

## 6. Review and ending the relationship
Outstanding lawful charges, unused balances, termination adjustments and credit notes require PB03. Provider suspension or closure must not defeat the learner-protection obligations in Annex E.
