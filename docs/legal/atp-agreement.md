## 1. Parties and relationship
1.1 LIV LLC, trading as Leadership Institute of Validation, headquartered in Virginia, United States, and the provider identified in Annex A propose this agreement for participation as a LIV Authorized Training Provider (ATP).
1.2 The relationship is limited to the scope expressly approved and activated by LIV. It is not government approval, external accreditation, an employment relationship, or authority to bind LIV to commitments to third parties.
1.3 Party particulars, authority to sign and formal notices remain AT01. A draft, payment, or acceptance pending activation does not grant training-reporting rights.

## 2. Documents and term
2.1 Annexes A–E form part of the agreement only when completed and adopted with an identifiable version. Linked rules must be identified by version at acceptance; a mutable link alone is insufficient to settle contractual incorporation.
2.2 Agreement start, duration, renewal, amendment notice and document precedence require AT02. No period or automatic extension is invented.
2.3 Acceptance must identify the active agreement version, provider, accepting representative and recorded event. Only the existing platform's authorized process can activate a completed version.

## 3. Authorization and activation
3.1 LIV assesses the application against the applicable criteria and records the decision. Assessment and decision are separated. The activation officer checks acceptance/conditions, an accepted active agreement, scope/trainer evidence, administrator two-step sign-in, billing particulars and checked test issuance.
3.2 Authorization covers only the approved entity, courses/versions, delivery modes, language, sites, trainers, dates and restrictions in Annex A. A broader field description is not approval of every course in that field.
3.3 New titles, additional capability, sites and relevant changes require the approved process. Neither payment nor a higher partnership level expands scope.

## 4. Provider obligations
4.1 Maintain lawful operation and any locally required permission, competent trainers, rights to materials, fit delivery arrangements and truthful completion evidence.
4.2 Explain the course and completion conditions, enrollment charges, complaint route and credential limits to learners. Do not offer inactive LIV qualifications or professional certifications.
4.3 Protect accounts and evidence, limit staff access to their roles, keep required records, respond to reviews, and report material changes under the approved policy.
4.4 Provide English application, import and issuance data. Do not alter names, dates or evidence to satisfy a technical check.

## 5. LIV responsibilities and issuance
5.1 LIV reviews within its approved procedures, records decisions and maintains the official credential record. The provider delivers training and submits completion information only; it does not issue, sign or modify LIV credentials.
5.2 The active award is the Training Completion Credential, without a LIV examination or expiry. All channels are subject to scope, status, data and duplication checks.
5.3 The [Credential Policy](credential-policy.md) governs dates, the recorded P-8 post-scope submission limit, corrections and revocation. Historical PDFs must not be silently rewritten.
5.4 Service availability and support commitments require AT03. No uninterrupted-service guarantee or acceptance guarantee is created.

## 6. Fees
6.1 Annex B identifies each approved charge, service, timing, currency and treatment on cancellation. Fees buy work, not a decision, level or standard.
6.2 A billing service hold may stop new issuance without changing authorization status or disabling existing verification. No debt automatically revokes a learner's credential.
6.3 P-6 remains open; fee proposals and internal comparison schedules are not incorporated as payable amounts.

## 7. Review, monitoring and cooperation
7.1 Cooperate with proportionate evidence checks and approved remote/on-site reviews. Protect learner confidentiality when supplying evidence.
7.2 Automated indicators can open a review case; they are not automatic guilt findings, suspension or revocation. Applicable indicators and human decisions are described in the [Program Rules](atp-program-rules.md).
7.3 Notice/access arrangements, review expenses and record access after termination require AT04. No unrestricted access to unrelated systems is granted.

## 8. Integrity, confidentiality and rights
8.1 Disclose conflicts, reject inducements and use LIV marks only under Annex C. No referral, purchase or gift may influence a quality decision.
8.2 Each party must protect nonpublic learner, commercial and review information and use it only for authorized purposes, subject to lawful disclosure. AT05 must settle disclosure, compelled-process and survival terms.
8.3 This agreement does not transfer ownership of provider curricula or third-party materials. Required evidence-use permissions and intellectual-property allocations require AT05.

## 9. Suspension, withdrawal and termination
9.1 Decisions must identify reasons, scope and effect. An open appeal does not restore rights automatically. A provider must stop representing suspended or withdrawn activities as currently authorized.
9.2 Grounds, notice, cure, emergency restrictions, reinstatement and termination rights require AT06. Technical administration controls are not a substitute for these terms.
9.3 Previously issued records are evaluated on their own evidence. Learners in unfinished training or pending issuance follow Annex E. Records, lawful obligations and complaints do not disappear on termination.

## 10. Disputes and liability
10.1 Complaints and appeals follow the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md).

10.3 No term excludes rights that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude.
