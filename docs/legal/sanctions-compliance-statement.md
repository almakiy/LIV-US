## 1. Purpose
LIV intends to conduct its activities consistently with applicable sanctions restrictions. This statement does not claim automated worldwide screening, governmental clearance or exemption from sanctions. Applicable laws and transaction-specific restrictions require qualified review.

## 2. Current records
The platform allows an authorized assessor to record the list checked, date, result and note, with an audit event. Possible matches enter the work queue. This is a record of a check performed by a person, not proof that a current official list was fetched or that all required parties/ownership interests were screened.

## 3. Review method
SN01 must approve official sources, screened parties, ownership/control analysis, timing and repeat checks. Gather sufficient relevant identifying information to distinguish namesakes, without unnecessary disclosure. Record sources and reasons rather than treating a name match as a final legal finding.

## 4. Possible matches
Escalate uncertain or confirmed restrictions to the designated owner and counsel. Do not proceed with a prohibited transaction or release/return potentially blocked funds merely because a routine commercial refund would otherwise apply. SN02 must define lawful holds, required reports and permissions. A main-account policy exception cannot waive law.

## 5. Communications and records
Protect screening information, record the outcome and inform affected parties to the extent lawful and appropriate. A screening concern does not silently revoke existing credentials. Retention follows the approved schedule.

## 6. Legal reference
[OFAC's official basic guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1501) describes program-specific prohibitions and authorizations. It is a legal-review reference, not a representation that LIV has adopted a particular automated provider, received a license or completed screening.
