## 1. Who decides what
LIV keeps the people who review a matter apart from the people who decide it. No one decides a matter they reviewed, and no one decides their own application.

| Area | Who reviews | Who decides | Built in the platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authorized Training Provider applications | Application Assessor | Authorization Decision-Maker | Yes: separate positions; activation is a third check by the Activation Officer |
| Partner levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold) | Level reviewer | Authorization Decision-Maker | Yes: levels rest on the quality record and cannot be bought |
| Credential issuance | The provider submits completion records | LIV issues the credential | Yes: the provider cannot issue without LIV |
| Revocation and corrections | LIV staff, with a recorded reason | LIV staff | Yes: every change is in the audit log; issued PDFs are never altered |
| Complaints and appeals | A person not involved in the original decision | [OWNER DECISION: name the appeals role or panel] | Case records exist; panel to be appointed |
| Exceptions to a rule | Staff prepare the request | The main account only, with reason, scope and period | Yes |
| Expert network applications | LIV staff | LIV staff, never the applicant | Yes, with impartiality flags |
| Library, newsletter and resources | One staff member | Another staff member | Yes: the author cannot publish their own item |

## 2. Roles
- **Application Assessor:** reviews applications against the published criteria. Cannot decide or activate.
- **Authorization Decision-Maker:** decides on applications. Cannot assess or activate.
- **Activation Officer:** confirms the activation checklist. Cannot assess or decide.
- **Partner Relations Coordinator:** first contact for applicants and partners. Holds no decision authority.
- **Main account:** approves terms, prices and genuine exceptions; does not assess.

Decisions, reasons and changes are kept in the audit log, which cannot be edited. Staff declare conflicts of interest every year. See the [Impartiality and Conflicts of Interest Statement](impartiality-statement.md) and the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md).
