## 1. Products
A paid gift funds a named person's membership. A LIV launch campaign grants membership within an approved campaign. A group package provides named seats. None creates a professional credential, organizational endorsement or ATP authorization. Membership rights follow the approved plan version.

## 2. Paid gift acceptance
The recipient must redeem a valid single-use code through a matching confirmed email. The permitted acceptance window is 90 days. GG01 must settle its exact starting event and treatment of delayed payment: current code sets the deadline at order creation, while the code becomes usable after payment. Resending rotates the code and does not create another gift or reset the stored deadline. No gift becomes an automatic paid subscription.

## 3. Membership period
Current gift logic can start a membership on redemption or after an existing active membership; a group seat ends with its package. These mechanics require GG02 contractual confirmation before a promise is published. Unredeemed/expired gifts and refunds require counsel approval; expiry does not silently establish forfeiture of statutory rights.

## 4. Group administration
The manager administers purchased seats, invitations and the organization's invoice. The approved privacy boundary for members is seat email and status only, not credentials, results or private account information. The current name display is gap G04. The manager may not use a member's password or take over the account.

Current code allows withdrawal/reassignment and ends an accepted seat immediately. I-11/GG03 must approve the contractual notice, member protections and transfer rules; availability of a button does not establish those rights. Individual accounts and independent credentials remain unaffected.

## 5. Launch grants and promotion
Staff issue grants only within an approved campaign's period, allocation and authority. Do not target people assessing LIV's own accreditation. Recipients are not required to provide favorable reviews. Promotional statements connected with a gift must accurately disclose the relationship where required. Do not imply that a recipient's employer endorses LIV.

## 6. Support and disputes
Use /contact or account support for invitation errors, alleged misuse and payment issues. Record decisions and reasons. Unresolved commercial provisions in GG01–GG04 block operational adoption of these draft terms.
