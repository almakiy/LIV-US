## 1. Proposal stage
The current platform accepts a title, summary, contribution category and name-display preference. Staff may accept or decline a proposal with a recorded note. Acceptance of a proposal is not a final publication contract, automatic endorsement or professional appointment.

## 2. Contributor responsibilities
Submit only material you are entitled to share for review. Identify third-party material, confidential information and relevant conflicts. Do not include personal or proprietary data without authority. State sources and distinguish evidence from opinion. English is the language of platform input.

## 3. Review permission
LIV needs limited permission to receive, store and evaluate a submission. CT01 must confirm this grant and the applicable retention terms. Submitting does not automatically transfer ownership or grant a perpetual unrestricted publication license.

## 4. Publication and attribution
Before publication, record the approved scope of use, attribution, editing, versions, distribution, commercial use, licensing term and any compensation. The current show-name choice addresses name display only. A separate accepted rights arrangement is needed under I-12/CT02. Do not invent a royalty, exclusivity term or assigned ownership.

## 5. Corrections and withdrawal
LIV and the contributor should identify factual errors, rights concerns and appropriate correction/retraction steps. CT03 must distinguish withdrawal before publication from handling already licensed/published copies. Preserve the review trail and protect confidential materials.

## 6. Independence
Contributing content does not grant ATP authorization, assessment authority, membership benefits not stated in a plan or an advantage in an application. Concerns follow the ordinary support and complaint process.
