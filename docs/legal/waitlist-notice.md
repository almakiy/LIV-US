## 1. Meaning
Joining is free. It is not a purchase, payment, reservation, admission, promised launch date or guaranteed place. Expected prices are estimates and not a binding offer. A later purchase requires available services and the applicable terms.

## 2. Information and contact
The form collects email and optional displayed profile fields, with permission to send the relevant opening message. That permission is distinct from newsletter and offers consent. The membership waitlist records the text and time of its choice. Do not infer permission for unrelated marketing.

## 3. Invitations and departure
The main account can send invitations for an available plan to waiting people in oldest-first batches. A confirmation contains a leave link. Leaving does not cancel a separate account or purchase. Invitations do not override eligibility requirements or guarantee a specific price.

## 4. Founding Member
Current code links this label to being on the particular plan's list before its first sale opening and later joining through a verified matching email. It is recognition only, without a discount, competence claim or decision advantage. General future-membership interest is not promised to qualify automatically.

## 5. Future assessments
Program interest does not establish assessment eligibility or activate a professional credential. No fees are collected through the interest-list mechanism. Data handling follows the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
