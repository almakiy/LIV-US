> This is a proposed initial draft for review, not an effective policy, legal advice, or evidence that a feature is live. Unresolved terms must be completed and approved before operational use. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not waived by this draft.

## 1. Scope and pricing
Charges must identify the service, approved price, USD currency, term and any applicable tax before a binding order. Expected waitlist prices are not offers. This draft does not adopt pending prices, refund percentages, cancellation windows or interest rates.

## 2. Methods and confirmation
Stripe Checkout processes card data on its hosted payment service; LIV does not store raw card details through that flow. LIV can record verified bank-transfer payments. Access tied to payment begins only after confirmed payment according to the service's rules. Payment never grants ATP authorization or a professional award.

## 3. Cancellation
An unpaid pending membership may be canceled through the existing flow. Declining renewal leaves the current paid period intact. Memberships and gifts do not renew automatically. Paid cancellation consequences require PY01; absence of a button does not eliminate mandatory cancellation rights.

## 4. Refund requests
Submit the order/invoice reference, issue and requested remedy through account support or /contact. LIV should verify the transaction, record the assessment and communicate the outcome under the adopted process. PY01 must define service-specific entitlement, refund timing, method, failed-service remedies and mandatory consumer exceptions. No blanket “non-refundable” term is imposed.

## 5. Distinct products
ATPs pay for identified application/review or other work, not acceptance; any non-refundable component remains P-6. Membership access, digital licenses, unused gifts, group-seat withdrawal and any future assessments have distinct delivery conditions. Do not reuse a refund rule across them without review.

## 6. Errors, disputes and accounting
Repeated provider notifications must not duplicate payment effects. Investigate duplicate charges, disputed invoices and chargebacks with supporting evidence. Refunds, credit notes and reconciliation must be recorded accurately. Current billing documentation says comprehensive refund/dispute mapping is not built; this draft does not claim otherwise. Voiding an unpaid invoice does not return money already received.

## 7. Consequences and tax
Reversing a payment may affect the related entitlement under approved terms, but must not automatically erase an independent credential. Tax treatment, invoice requirements, bank/FX costs and unclaimed gift value require PY02. No tax exemption or universal invoice compliance is asserted.
