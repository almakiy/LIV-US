## 1. Prohibition
Do not offer, request, accept or channel money, gifts, favors or another benefit to improperly influence authorization, review, issuance, procurement or other business decisions. Do not use intermediaries or disguised fees to do what would be improper directly.

## 2. Commercial separation
Approved fees pay for services; they do not buy a favorable outcome or exemption from quality criteria. Membership, a higher partnership level, sponsorship or resource contribution must not influence substantive decisions. Declare relevant conflicts and follow the [Impartiality Statement](impartiality-statement.md).

## 3. Gifts and campaigns
Launch memberships must stay within an approved campaign and must not target people reviewing or deciding LIV's accreditation. Do not require favorable publicity or conceal a promotional relationship. Hospitality, donations, sponsorships and any permitted courtesy limits remain AB01; no value threshold is invented.

## 4. Records
Describe expenditures and receipts accurately. Do not falsify invoices, hide recipients, backdate decisions or use an exception to erase a prohibited benefit. Preserve evidence and restrict access to sensitive reports.

## 5. Reports and investigation
Use the approved internal reporting route or /contact to report suspected improper influence. AB02 must establish confidential handling, conflicts in investigation, safeguards against retaliation, consequences and any reporting duties. A good-faith allegation requires fair review; it is not a predetermined finding.

## 6. Legal assessment
Counsel must assess U.S. and other applicable anti-bribery requirements, including relevant foreign-official dealings. [U.S. Department of Justice FCPA guidance](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/foreign-corrupt-practices-act) is a reference, not a claim that this statement is a complete compliance program or that every statutory provision applies identically to LIV.
