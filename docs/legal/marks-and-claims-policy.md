## 1. Ownership and limits
LIV's name, seal and supplied marks identify LIV LLC and its services. This draft does not assert trademark registration. Approved use must accurately describe the actual relationship and scope. No use may imply government endorsement, external accreditation, ISO conformity, professional licensure or guaranteed employment.

## 2. ATP description
Use the full designation “LIV Authorized Training Provider (ATP)” only for current authorization and the activities actually covered. A provider delivers training and reports completion; LIV issues the credential. Display current scope restrictions and verification access where the approved license requires them. Do not extend a branch's, trainer's or course's authorization by association.

## 3. Partnership badges
Use only the Bronze, Silver or Gold level actually recorded under the existing scheme. Do not invent eligibility, thresholds or promotion rights. The provider badge belongs on approved provider-facing displays and verification presentation, not the learner's certificate. Exact artwork, permitted media, alteration rules and withdrawal duties remain P-16/MK01. Pending artwork is not permission to create a new official seal.

## 4. Individual membership
A membership card/badge states membership, not professional certification or competence. A member may truthfully share the membership verification link and identify LIV membership in an appropriate profile section. Founding Member is a recognition label without a discount or additional decision rights. Inactive memberships must not be represented as current.

## 5. Other references
A learner may accurately describe the issued Training Completion Credential. A resource contribution or event attendance does not make the contributor an approved assessor or the attendee professionally certified. Reference to another organization's framework does not imply its endorsement.

## 6. Misuse
LIV records suspected misuse, requests correction through the approved procedure, and may take supported contractual measures. Genuine evidence and status history remain intact. Appeals follow the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md). MK02 must settle notice and cure details rather than inventing deadlines.
