## 1. Truthful delivery
Deliver only the approved activities with eligible trainers and the authorized means. Keep records that substantiate each reported completion. Never fabricate attendance, identity, completion, dates, qualifications or permissions.

## 2. Learners
Explain the provider's role and LIV's issuance role. Give clear enrollment, completion, fees, refund and complaint information. Handle accessibility needs and complaints fairly. Do not require a learner to buy LIV membership to obtain an issued record or public verification.

## 3. Marketing
Use current scope and the recorded badge only under the approved license. Do not claim external/government approval, ISO conformity, unlimited course authorization or professional status from a completion credential. Do not imply that payment, volume or level guarantees acceptance.

## 4. Integrity
Disclose interests and reject bribes, secret inducements and altered evidence. Do not pressure reviewers, retaliate against complainants or conceal material changes. Cooperation with an evidence request is not permission to expose unrelated personal data.

## 5. Systems and records
Use named authorized accounts, role-appropriate access and required administrator two-step sign-in. Protect learner data, follow the approved retention schedule and report security concerns. Do not share credentials, bypass billing/scope checks or repeatedly alter inputs to force acceptance.

## 6. Accountability
ATPs remain responsible for personnel and arrangements covered by their authorization. Subcontracting or additional sites cannot expand scope silently. LIV records alleged breaches, considers evidence and uses the adopted agreement's remedies. The [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md) remains available.
