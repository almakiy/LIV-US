## 1. Program scope
Only the active Training Completion Credential is covered. LIV issues; the ATP trains and reports completion. Acceptance is separate from activation. Payment, an open appeal or a partnership level does not authorize additional activities.

## 2. Criteria
| Code | Required subject and evidence |
|---|---|
| C1 | Legal entity, accountability, representative authority and locally required training permission |
| C2 | Accountable academic lead and relevant management capability |
| C3 | Eligible trainers, qualifications, subject/delivery evidence and verified findings per scope |
| C4 | Course outline/version, outcomes, mapping and rights to materials |
| C5 | Demonstrated delivery ability for the requested site/platform, mode and language |
| C6 | Completion rules and traceable attendance/participation evidence appropriate to the active completion credential |
| C7 | Enrollment/refund information, complaints and learner-protection arrangements |
| C8 | Records, access, retention, lawful sharing and incident handling |
| C9 | Integrity, interests, marketing and intermediary/subcontractor controls |

A scoring average cannot compensate for a failed mandatory criterion. Missing information requires clarification, not an invented pass. P-5 trainer thresholds remain AP01. C6 does not introduce an exam for completion credentials.

## 3. Application and decision
The journey is draft, submitted, completeness check, technical review and awaiting decision, with information/corrective-action loops. Submission locks a version; amendments form new versions. Decisions are reasoned, separated from assessment, and can accept pending activation or decline; withdrawal/closure are recorded. Recalculation preserves earlier recommendations and must use current evidence.

## 4. Activation and scope
Complete the checklist in the agreement, including an active accepted agreement and eligible trainer evidence. Titles must be approved within actual capability; no free-title issuance. The approved scope records entity, course/version, delivery limits, sites, trainers, dates and restrictions. Renewal is a review request, not a payment effect.

## 5. Levels
The existing scheme uses Bronze, Silver and Gold. It does not create different minimum quality standards or professional-certification rights. Only the recorded level may be shown. P-15 promotion thresholds, P-16 license and P-17 Gold commitments remain open; no proposed months, sample counts or fees are adopted here.

## 6. Monitoring
The implemented automatic checks include a batch at least 5 times the prior 12-full-month monthly issuance average where history exists, a batch exceeding half the included allowance, 3 refused batches in 90 days, and 3 complaints in 90 days. Cases request response in 5 working days and escalate after 10 without response. There is no automatic large-batch hold, revocation or guilt finding.

Other concerns described in the authorization document include contradictions, late corrections, suspected false records, verification anomalies, unreported change and misleading claims. The inspected indicator module does not implement every described detector. Human review remains necessary; AP02 must resolve the wording/detector differences.

## 7. Change, transition and exceptions
Material changes and renewal follow their approved policies. P-9 and P-11 remain AP03 despite conflicting later source entries. A genuine exception requires the main account's recorded, bounded decision; normal alternatives and specialist judgment remain with authorized staff. Exceptions cannot invent facts, remove indispensable quality conditions, violate rights or enable inactive award types.

## 8. Review rights
Explain findings and allow responses and appeals under the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md). Preserve criteria versions, evidence and the audit trail.
