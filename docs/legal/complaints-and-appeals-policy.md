## 1. Scope
A complaint concerns service, conduct or records. An appeal asks for review of a specified decision affecting the requester. Neither a complaint nor an appeal itself restores issuance rights, reverses a decision or proves misconduct.

## 2. Submission
Use /contact or signed-in support, identify the issue/decision, provide relevant evidence, and state the desired remedy. Avoid unnecessary third-party personal information. Public contact complaints/appeals are linked to QMS cases; routing of individual support messages into that workflow must be verified before promising automatic case creation for every channel.

## 3. Recording and timing
Record receipt, case number, responsible handler, evidence and events. Current QMS targets are acknowledgment within 5 business days and decision within 30 calendar days of receipt. The code counts Monday–Friday as business days without a holiday calendar. CA01 must confirm calendar, extensions and delay notifications. These are response targets, not a guarantee of a favorable outcome.

## 4. Investigation and independence
Give relevant parties a fair opportunity to respond while limiting confidential information. Record conflicts and reassign where necessary. An appeal reviewer must be independent of the handler and original decision-maker. Current code checks names; substantive independence requires human verification. Preserve evidence and reasons rather than overwriting prior decisions.

## 5. Outcome
State whether upheld, partially upheld, not upheld or withdrawn, with reasons, remedies, responsible people and any available further route. Corrective action closure requires recorded root cause, action and verification of effectiveness. Do not retaliate against a good-faith requester or require membership for access to this process.

## 6. Fees and legal rights
Complaints are free. Any appeal fee or refund rule remains subject to P-6 and CA02; no provisional figure is adopted. Access to regulators or courts and non-waivable rights is not removed by the internal process. Retain cases under the approved retention schedule.
