## 1. Private organization
LIV LLC, trading as Leadership Institute of Validation, is a private organization headquartered in Virginia, United States. It is not a government agency or regulatory authority. It does not claim government, federal, ISO or other external accreditation or recognition.

## 2. Current credentials
LIV issues the Training Completion Credential on an eligible ATP's completion report within its authorized scope. It has no LIV examination and no expiry date. It is not a professional qualification, professional certification or license. The Assessed Professional Qualification and Professional Certification are not enabled.

## 3. Future objectives
Any reference to ISO/IEC 17024:2026 concerns a future target for the separate professional-certification track. It is not a statement of current accreditation or conformity. An inactive external pathway would concern awards owned and issued by another body; it is not an additional LIV-issued credential.

## 4. Meaning of other services
Membership and Founding Member recognition do not establish competence. A free waitlist is not a purchase or reserved place. A listing of an ATP describes its recorded relationship and scope, not unlimited authorization for every course it offers. A successful integrity check does not establish governmental acceptance.

## 5. Use in decisions
Employers, learners and other recipients should consider the credential's actual type, scope and current verification result for their intended purpose. LIV does not promise a job, academic credit, licensing acceptance or recognition by another body. This does not exclude responsibility for an inaccurate description or any mandatory right.
