## 1. Understand what is offered
Before enrollment, a learner should be told who delivers training, the relevant authorized course/scope, who issues the credential, the completion conditions, and the provider's charges and complaint route. LIV issues the credential; a provider cannot promise a professional award through the current completion pathway.

## 2. Access and accuracy
Basic access to an issued record and public verification are free and independent of membership. Secure account matching protects records from unauthorized claims. A learner may request correction with supporting information and receive a reasoned handling of the request under the [Credential Policy](credential-policy.md).

## 3. Data and fair treatment
Use the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for data choices and applicable rights. Optional marketing must not be a condition of accessing an issued credential. A learner may seek accessible assistance without assuming that all requested accommodations are already available.

## 4. Complaints
A learner can contact LIV directly about its services or relevant partner conduct, even without paid membership. The [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md) supplies the process and QMS targets. Good-faith complaints must not be treated as evidence of misconduct by the learner.

## 5. Suspension or closure of a provider
A provider's later suspension does not automatically invalidate an earlier credential. LIV must distinguish existing holders from people whose training or issuance is unfinished. P-13/LR01 must define information, evidence transfer, alternative completion, costs, refunds and support responsibilities. This draft does not promise substitute training, compensation or issuance without evidence.

## 6. Continuity
Account or membership changes must not erase the official record. Retention and lawful restrictions follow the applicable policies. Keep the original credential and verification link; report discrepancies rather than altering the document.
