> Proposed draft. It sets out how a LIV digital badge may be shown and what happens when it is misused. It is not published until the open points are completed and the main account approves it.

## 1. What a badge is
A LIV digital badge is a signed record of a credential or membership that anyone can check. It shows what LIV recorded at the time it was checked. It is informational only. It is not a licence to practise, not a professional qualification unless a named scheme states one, and not an endorsement of the holder's competence.

## 2. What you may do
- Show the badge on your profiles, CV, email signature, website and proposals.
- Link the badge to its verification page so that others can check its status.
- Describe the record accurately, using the credential or membership name shown on the badge.

## 3. What you must not do
- Change, edit, recreate or forge a badge, its image or its data.
- Present a completion record as a professional qualification, certification, accreditation or licence.
- Present a membership as a qualification or as a sign of competence.
- Show a badge after it has been revoked or after a membership has ended.
- Use the badge in a way that suggests LIV approves a product, service, person or claim beyond the record.
- Use another person's badge or share it as if it were yours.

## 4. Status and checking
Each badge is checked against LIV's records when it is requested. A revoked credential returns no badge, and a membership that has ended returns no badge. A status is shown with its check time. Anyone can check a badge at the verification page.

## 5. Reporting misuse
Anyone who believes a badge is being misused may report it through the contact page, choosing the badge misuse topic. Reports must identify the badge and explain the concern, with evidence where possible. Reports can be made without an account. LIV acknowledges each report in writing and records the outcome.

## 6. How LIV responds to misuse
1. LIV reviews the report with someone who was not involved in issuing the record.
2. Where misuse is confirmed, LIV may ask the holder to stop, remove the badge reference or correct the claim.
3. If the holder does not comply, or the misuse is serious, LIV may revoke the record or suspend the membership, and may publish that it has done so.
4. The holder may appeal under the Complaints and Appeals Policy.

## 7. Changes to badges
A badge's content may change when the record changes (for example, a new status or scope). LIV does not alter an issued record's facts. Corrections are logged.
