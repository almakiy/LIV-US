## 1. Account purpose
An individual account supports personal credentials, membership, purchases, preferences and support. It does not confer ATP rights or access to other people's information. Use accurate English data. Age/guardian requirements remain IA01.

## 2. Authentication
Keep passwords, recovery links and verification tokens private. Confirm control of your email address before services requiring verification. Two-step sign-in is available; actual mandatory settings must not be overstated. Report lost access or suspected compromise through /contact.

## 3. Linking credentials
A record can be linked through a confirmed email matching its privately recorded recipient email, or a staff-reviewed claim with a written reason. A public number or matching name alone is insufficient. One record links to one account at a time. Linking or releasing a link does not alter the official record or its historical PDF.

## 4. Information changes
Profile edits do not silently correct issued credentials. Use the separate correction process for those records. Staff may ask for proportionate evidence for ownership claims; do not submit unnecessary full identity documents through ordinary messages.

## 5. Membership and sponsorship
Paid membership is optional. Expiry does not remove basic credential access. A gift or group seat does not transfer ownership of your account to its payer. Changes to sponsored membership follow the approved group terms while your personal account and independent records remain yours.

## 6. Privacy and closure
Channel choices follow the [Communications Policy](communications-and-consent-policy.md). An account export and closure request are available. A closure request is reviewed; it does not immediately erase credentials, lawful financial records or audit evidence. I-2/IA02 must set the process, retention and alternative access after closure.

## 7. Restrictions and concerns
Misuse may be investigated under the approved site terms. Report account/claim problems through support. The [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md) provides review without requiring paid membership.
