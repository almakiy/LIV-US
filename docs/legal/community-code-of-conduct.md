## 1. Participation
Use community spaces for relevant, respectful professional exchange. Access depends on the event's announced audience, capacity and registration rules. Registration does not establish professional certification, approved expertise or a right to speak for LIV.

## 2. Expected behavior
Do not harass, discriminate unlawfully, threaten, impersonate, spam, disclose private information or distribute unlawful material. Critique ideas respectfully. Disclose relevant commercial interests and do not present unverified claims as LIV's position. Protect confidential employer, client and learner information.

## 3. Joining links and recordings
Joining links are available to registered participants. Do not redistribute restricted links. Recording, screenshots, participant-list disclosure and use of a person's name/image require the announced permissions and CC01. Attendance alone is not consent to publicity or reuse of a presentation.

## 4. Moderation
Hosts may identify concerns and refer them to the responsible LIV staff. Document removal/restriction decisions and use measures proportionate to the problem; emergency protections and review rights require CC02. Membership status or spending does not exempt anyone from conduct rules.

## 5. Reporting
Use /contact or account support with relevant event details and evidence. Limit unnecessary disclosure of other participants' information. Formal complaints follow the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md).

## 6. Event changes
Dates, hosts, tools, cancellations and any paid-event remedies require I-9/CC03 before announcement. This draft creates no undisclosed recording license, attendance credit or automatic refund exclusion.
