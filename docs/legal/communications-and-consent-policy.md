## 1. Channels
Necessary service communications concern account security, credentials, applications, payments, memberships and relevant operational changes. The knowledge newsletter and commercial offers are separate optional channels. Waitlist opening messages use their own recorded permission. No channel is implied by buying membership or receiving a partner-issued invitation.

## 2. Recording choices
Newsletter and offers choices are recorded separately with the text shown, source, time and grant/withdrawal. Topic choices refine the newsletter audience. Optional choices must not be preselected or merged into a general privacy acknowledgment.

## 3. Withdrawal
Use account preferences or the channel's unsubscribe link. Stopping one marketing channel does not terminate membership, remove basic credential access or automatically withdraw another independent choice. Necessary service messages remain eligible for sending; delivery cannot be guaranteed where an address fails or email is unconfigured. Do not disguise promotion as a service notice.

## 4. Newsletter operations
Draft, review and sending roles follow the platform workflow; sending requires its launch control and main-account action. Approval does not establish the truth of unsupported claims. I-8/CM01 must settle editorial responsibility, footer and working delivery infrastructure.

## 5. Timing and delivery gaps
Consent is checked when newsletter recipients are queued. CM02 requires verification that withdrawal also suppresses already-queued marketing before delivery. Do not promise instant removal from every queue without evidence. Service delivery failures and suppression handling must not be confused with marketing withdrawal.

## 6. Records and concerns
Retain consent evidence under the approved schedule, proportionately to its purpose. Requests and complaints follow the privacy/support routes. This document does not assert universal legal compliance merely because a consent log exists.
