## 1. Principle
Fees pay for described work and benefits, never favorable quality decisions. Membership, a gift, partnership level, referral or commercial relationship gives no advantage in eligibility, assessment or award decisions. No future assessment program is activated by this statement.

## 2. Decision roles
The ATP assessor and authorization decision-maker must be different people. Activation is a separate check. Commercial/support staff do not gain decision authority by handling a purchase. The main account approves genuine exceptions within the approved limits; it does not manufacture evidence or competence.

## 3. Conflicts
Personnel should disclose relevant financial, employment, family, advisory, referral or other interests and update a declaration when circumstances change. QMS records annual declarations. Assignments must be assessed for actual independence and reassigned where needed; a different displayed name alone is insufficient.

## 4. Decision paths
Distinguish normal procedure, published alternatives, specialist interpretation and genuine exceptions. Recalculation preserves previous recommendations and input evidence; it must not hide a departure from a rule. Exceptions need a reason, defined scope and duration/usage limit and remain traceable. They cannot change facts, breach law or activate unavailable credential types.

## 5. Gifts and oversight
Launch membership gifts may not target people assessing or deciding LIV's own accreditation application. Commercial campaigns require their approved controls. Report pressure, undisclosed conflicts or inducements through /contact or the approved internal route. See the [Anti-Bribery Statement](anti-bribery-statement.md).

## 5a. Experts, trainers and assessment
People who join the LIV Expert Network declare their conflicts of interest when they apply and update the declaration when it changes. Staff see a flag when one person asks for roles that should stay apart. When assessment programs operate:
- A person who writes assessment questions does not train candidates in the same domain for two years (owner decision, October 26, 2026).
- A trainer does not supervise, mark or decide the assessment of their own learners.
- A member of the appeals panel does not hear an appeal about a decision they took part in, or about their own learners.
Approval into the network records a role only; access to assessment material is granted separately, by name, and logged.

## 6. Limitations and review
Current QMS configuration can permit documented self-approval of some controlled documents/corrective actions. This draft does not falsely promise universal two-person approval. IM01 must settle that configuration and actual oversight appointments. A records system is not proof that a committee has met or an external standard is satisfied.
