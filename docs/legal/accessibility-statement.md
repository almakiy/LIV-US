## 1. Target
LIV aims for accessible digital services using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its target. This is not a declaration of complete conformity, independent certification, or provider-wide accessibility.

## 2. Evidence and limitations
Project documents describe automated axe-core checks on specified end-to-end pages. Automated tests cannot establish complete accessibility. Manual, assistive-technology, PDF and external-service reviews remain AX01; no new production audit is represented here.

## 3. Assistance
Use /contact or signed-in support to identify the affected page/document, task and preferred accessible format. Browser/assistive-technology details may help. Avoid unnecessary medical information. AX02 must establish an alternative channel for anyone unable to use the form.

## 4. Handling barriers
Record the issue, consider an accessible alternative, assign responsibility and explain available assistance. Help with forms must preserve privacy, identity and accuracy. Response targets and accommodations require AX02. ATP delivery arrangements and future assessment accommodations require their own applicable rules.

## 5. Review
Unresolved concerns follow the [Complaints and Appeals Policy](complaints-and-appeals-policy.md). Publication requires a verified review scope, date, known limitations and remediation owners.
