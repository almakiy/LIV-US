# Course Quality Standard for Authorized Training Providers

- **Status:** DRAFT – NOT LEGAL ADVICE – PENDING COUNSEL REVIEW

- **Owner:** LIV LLC
- **Applies to:** Authorized Training Providers, their course authors and trainers, and LIV staff who approve courses

> Proposed draft. It sets out what LIV expects of a course that is delivered under an Authorized Training Provider's scope and what LIV checks before a course is listed. It is not an accreditation. LIV does not accredit courses under any external CPD or professional scheme, and no course may describe itself as accredited, approved or recognised for CPD or continuing professional development hours unless a named external body has granted that accreditation in writing.

## 1. What a course is for
A course must state the skill or knowledge it adds, the role or working situation it serves, and what the learner can do differently afterwards. A course that cannot state this is not listed.

## 2. Required information for every course
Each course submitted to LIV for listing under a scope must include:
1. **Title.** One title per course. A change of title is a change to the course and must be notified to LIV before it is used.
2. **Learning objectives.** At least five, each a short statement of what the learner will know or be able to do. Objectives are the basis of the record: they appear in the digital badge criteria when the course is completed.
3. **Audience.** The roles or levels the course is for, and the knowledge or skill the learner should already have.
4. **Delivery.** Format (in person, online or blended), duration in days or hours, maximum group size, and the language of delivery. Delivery in a language other than English is recorded but the record itself is kept in English.
5. **Agenda.** A timed agenda showing the sequence of sessions and assessments.
6. **Materials.** The list of materials given to learners: slides, handouts, quizzes, case studies and recordings. Materials are supplied by the provider and must be the version LIV was shown.
7. **Authors and trainers.** The people who wrote the course and the people who deliver it, with their qualifications and the scope for which each is qualified.
8. **Policies.** The provider's policies that apply to the course: health and safety, equal opportunities, data protection and disability discrimination.
9. **Welcome information.** The documents and registration information sent to learners on booking.

## 3. How learning is checked
Every course includes at least one way for the learner to show understanding during the course: a quiz, an assignment, a practical exercise or a structured group discussion. The course states which one it uses and when. A course with none is not listed. Completion is recorded only when the provider has delivered the course; attendance alone is not a completion.

## 4. Continuing development
The course states what the learner might do after training to apply it and to keep developing, for example a follow-up task in the workplace, a review after three months, or a link to a further course in the same scope. LIV does not state or promise any number of professional development hours.

## 5. Keeping the course current
1. The provider reviews each course at least once a year and whenever a standard, law or practice it relies on changes.
2. Each change is recorded with its date, the reason and the person who approved it, and kept for the period in the Records Retention Schedule.
3. A material change (new objectives, new trainer, new scope, new delivery format) is notified to LIV before it is used. LIV may ask for a new review.
4. A course that is no longer delivered is withdrawn from LIV's listing.

## 6. Feedback and evaluation
1. Each course collects feedback from every learner, covering the content, the trainer, the materials and whether the learning is applicable.
2. The provider measures impact where it can, for example through a follow-up question after three months about the task the course was for. Measures are used to improve the course.
3. Feedback summaries are sent to LIV each year and reviewed with the course record. A pattern of low feedback leads to a review of the course.

## 7. How LIV checks a course
1. **Completeness.** A course without the items in section 2 is returned, not refused, with the missing items listed.
2. **Consistency.** LIV checks that the course matches the scope the provider holds. A course outside the scope is not listed.
3. **Objectives and checks.** LIV checks that the objectives are measurable and that section 3 is met.
4. **Records.** LIV records the decision, the reasons and the version reviewed. Decisions are made by a staff member who did not write or deliver the course.
5. **Sampling.** LIV may review a sample of delivered courses, materials and feedback at any time.

## 8. What a learner receives
A learner who completes a listed course receives the Training Completion Credential, which records completion only. It shows the course title, the provider, the completion date and the learning objectives. It is not a professional qualification, not a certification and not a CPD record.

## 9. Changes to this standard
LIV publishes changes to this standard with a version number and an effective date. Changes apply to courses listed after the effective date. A course already listed continues under the version it was approved under until its next review.
