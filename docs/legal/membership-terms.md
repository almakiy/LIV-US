## 1. Nature of membership
Membership is optional and is not a credential, proof of competence, occupational license or ATP authorization. It grants only the benefits described in the approved plan version. It gives no advantage in eligibility, assessment or award decisions.

## 2. Plans and availability
The repository names LIV Member, LIV Member Plus, Student Membership and Corporate Membership Package. Use only a plan approved and opened for sale with available benefits. Final binding plan values remain I-4/MB01 in this drafting instruction; the October 23 approval entry and older pending lists must be reconciled, not silently resolved here. Student eligibility requires I-5.

## 3. Purchase and activation
The purchaser must see the applicable plan, price in USD, period, benefits and terms before committing. Full confirmed payment can activate membership once; it does not issue a credential. The recorded plan snapshot identifies the purchase terms. Other charges follow the [Payment and Refund Policy](payment-and-refund-policy.md).

## 4. Term and renewal
Membership does not renew automatically. A renewal is a new purchase. Choosing “I will not renew” does not end the paid period early. The system is designed to send a reminder 30 days before expiry unless renewal is declined, subject to functioning email delivery. An uncompleted pending purchase may be canceled; paid cancellation and refund rights remain MB02.

## 5. Benefits and card
Benefits are limited to those actually included and available. A membership discount changes the commercial price, not substantive quality requirements. The card and verification link state membership only; name visibility is optional. Founding Member is a recognition label without a discount or decision privilege. Follow the [Marks and Claims Policy](marks-and-claims-policy.md).

## 6. End or suspension
Expiry ends time-limited membership benefits, not independently held credentials or separately purchased rights. Staff suspension requires a reason; contractual grounds, notice, restoration and any financial consequence require MB03. No automatic right to replace a purchased benefit with an inferior one is adopted.

## 7. Help
Use account support for billing, benefit or card issues. A complaint does not require an active paid membership.
