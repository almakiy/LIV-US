# Curriculum source maps

Status (October 16, 2026): owner input, kept for later phases. These are **source maps**, not LIV curricula. They list laws, standards, guidance, courses, qualifications and professional credentials that LIV will study when it builds its own body of knowledge and competence standard (Track S, S.2). Nothing here is published on the site, and nothing here describes or defines a LIV credential. The HSE Governance scheme stays IN DEVELOPMENT.

## How these maps are used
1. **Benchmark, not copy.** Other bodies' syllabi, bodies of knowledge and exam outlines are studied for coverage and level only. Their text, structure tables, names and trademarks are not reproduced. Each body's terms decide what may be quoted (see `REFERENCE-FRAMEWORKS.md` §1).
2. **Verify before use.** Every edition and date is re-checked when a curriculum unit is written. ISO editions are checked with `npm run check:standards`. Items in the map that are not yet in the standards registry: IEC 31010, ISO 30422, ISO 22301, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 7101. Claims in the map that LIV has not yet verified itself (for example "confirmed in 2026" for ISO 37000 and ISO 37301, the FDA QMSR date and the NEBOSH 2026 specification) stay marked as unverified until checked against the issuer.
3. **Law stays separate from courses.** The map's own rule is kept: a law or regulation sets a duty; a standard sets requirements; guidance explains; a course trains; a qualification assesses; a credential needs experience and maintenance.
4. **LIV names stay distinct.** No LIV unit or credential takes the name or abbreviation of a course or credential listed in a map.

## Research contributions
External researchers may contribute research for the library under the program in `BUILD-PLAN.md` Track S (S.7): a published call for topics, a submission template, conflict-of-interest declaration, double review, the author's name on the published item, and a license the author agrees to.
