# Decision register and operating rules (single source for what is decided, proposed or replaced)

Updated October 20, 2026. This is the one active reference for the additions to the ATP track. Older proposal documents are history; where they disagree with this file, this file wins. Every requirement carries its source: **O** owner direction, **P** proposal awaiting the owner, **C** contract or counsel, **S** target standard, **L** law once its application is confirmed, **X** optional practice. A proposal does not become a rule by being written into a specification or sent to a builder.

## 1. Fixed by the owner
- LIV issues the three credential types the owner defined. Only the Training Completion Credential is live: no exam, no expiry. Batches A to I concern that type. The Assessed Professional Qualification, the Professional Certification and the External Pathway are not enabled and cannot be granted from the ATP track.
- English is the only language for application, import and issuance data and for certificates. Text is checked, never translated or converted.
- ISO/IEC 17024:2026 is the reference target for the future Professional Certification; no claim of accreditation or conformity is made. Training and certification stay separate.
- US dollar is the platform currency.
- The partnership levels are Bronze (entry), Silver and Gold; the badge shows on a provider's page, the register and a small badge on the verification page, never on a learner's certificate.
- Certificate design (templates by level, seals, attendance certificates) is parked.
- Only the **main account** (the super admin account that holds no staff position) approves an exception.
- Audits open on indicators (contradiction, sudden change, repeated errors, many complaints); the audit thresholds are approved.
- No hold on large batches: flag and sample.

## 2. Four decision paths (they must not be mixed)
| Path | Example | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Normal procedure | Accepting complete evidence; adding a title that matches the criteria | The staff member the position allows |
| Published alternative | A platform log instead of a paper attendance sheet, where the policy allows it | The authorized staff member; the alternative and its reason are recorded |
| Interpretation or specialist opinion | Whether a specialist subject falls inside the provider's capability | A competent reviewer gives the opinion; the decision follows the normal authority |
| Genuine exception | A departure from a rule or time limit that no published alternative allows | The main account only; staff prepare, they do not approve |

A reviewer's professional judgement that the policy already delegates is not an exception. Exceeding a binding limit is never hidden under "recalculate" or "correct".

## 3. Exceptions (built)
Prepared by any staff account; approved or refused by the main account only. An approval covers one rule, one case and a bounded time or number of uses. It never proves a criterion that was not met, never changes real dates, evidence or the original score, never moves to another case and never renews itself. Outside the authority of any exception: inventing a completion, identity, licence or recognition; granting a credential type that is not enabled; dropping a quality requirement a valid credential depends on; breaking a law or another party's rights; showing a settlement that did not happen; hiding a material conflict or erasing a decision. Repeated exceptions on the same rule are flagged so the rule, the guidance or a published alternative can change. If the main account is absent, normal procedures and published alternatives continue and genuine exceptions wait. A change of the main account's ownership is its own procedure, not a way round absence.

## 4. Continuity of work
Every item of work has an owner position, a waiting reason and an age. The work queue (Admin > Work) lists what waits for which position and shows when a position is vacant, so work is reassigned instead of depending on one person's memory. Scheduled events retry without repeating a decision or an issuance. A service outage uses a pre-authorized alternative that keeps the real time and the evidence; it is not a new exception each time.

## 5. Records and truth (proposal P, needs counsel before it is applied)
A historical credential's file and history are kept and never regenerated. A suspended provider or a debt does not revoke a credential automatically. When evidence shows a material error in an earlier record, a case is opened; nothing declares it valid or fraudulent on suspicion. A proven error is handled by a statement and correction policy that protects honesty and rights: it never confirms an event that is proven false and never hides the history.

## 6. Commercial rules
Fees pay for described services, never for acceptance, a level, a score or a renewal. Published offers and discounts are ordinary procedure; any financial exception goes through the main account. The reference prices from the IACET webinar (2025) are an internal provisional base for finance only: no price is published or charged until the owner approves. Fee proposals are in `docs/FEES-REVIEW.md`.

## 7. Status of the build (October 20, 2026)
Built and tested: application (staged, English-only, fee at submission), review and separated decision, activation, positions, title and scope-expansion requests, trainer additions, email, levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold) with reviews, exceptions, public register with search and an application guide.
Built (Batch H, October 20): monitoring indicators and audit cases (thresholds as approved; the response and escalation periods are 5 and 10 working days), renewals, material-change notices, sanctions screening records, standards versions, the records retention schedule, reminders for drafts idle for 30 and 90 days, and automated WCAG 2.2 AA checks (axe-core) in the end-to-end suite. Pending decisions that the build leaves open: P-9 (applications already open keep their version), P-11 (renewal period: dates are set by hand), P-12 (categories and materiality of changes), P-14 (retention periods).
Proposed and not built: the seal and template families (parked); a full manual accessibility audit (the automated check covers the pages listed in the end-to-end suite only).
