# LIV Principles (draft, not published)

Status: draft for owner review, October 25, 2026. Not linked from the site. No claim here may be published until each line is true in operation.

## Why these principles
LIV records, verifies and recognizes learning. People rely on our records only if we are impartial, clear and dependable. These principles guide staff, experts, committee members and partners.

| Principle | What it means | What it looks like | What we avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impartiality | Decisions rest on evidence, never on payment, volume or relationships | Review is separated from decision; conflicts of interest are declared; levels cannot be bought | Letting sales, friendship or pressure change a decision |
| Transparency | People can see how we decide and what our records mean | Published rules, dated policy versions, public verification, stated limits of each credential | Vague claims, hidden criteria, inflated figures |
| Accuracy | Every record is correct and traceable | Records are never silently changed; corrections are logged with a reason | Rewriting history, guessing, unchecked data |
| Clarity | Simple words, few steps | Plain English, one clear next step, what a credential is and is not | Jargon, unclear instructions, implied accreditation |
| Service | We answer and we close what we open | Stated response times; the person who receives a request owns it until it is closed | "Not my task", passing people around |
| Privacy | Personal data serves its stated purpose only | Separate consents, minimum public data, data export and closure on request | Selling data, marketing without consent |
| Improvement | We fix causes, not symptoms | Complaints and appeals feed changes; recurring issues get a root-cause fix | Hiding errors, "we have always done it this way" |

## Do and don't
- Do: "Here is how the decision was made and how to appeal." Don't: "The system does not allow it."
- Do: "This credential confirms completion of training; it is not a professional certification." Don't: "Internationally accredited."
- Do: "I will check and come back to you by Thursday." Don't: "Please email support."

## Limits
LIV is not accredited under ISO/IEC 17024 or any other standard, and these principles make no conformity claim.
