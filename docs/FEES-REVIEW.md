# LIV ATP fees: benchmark and proposal (draft for owner approval, P-6)

Status (October 18, 2026): **proposal only.** Nothing here is applied to the price catalog, the public fee page or any invoice. It refines `docs/FEES.md` (owner-approved October 5) for the Authorized Training Provider (ATP) track after Batches A to E. Every number is in US dollars. Benchmarks come from the owner's report on comparable approval schemes (centre, provider and sponsor approvals by NOCN, TQUK, Highfield, City & Guilds, Pearson, BCS, PMI, APMG, PeopleCert, CPD Standards Office, The CPD Group, NASBA, SHRM, HRCI, ANAB, UKAS). Where a benchmark is in pounds it is quoted in pounds; no conversion is used to justify a number. Public pages from those bodies were not re-checked, so treat the benchmarks as the report's, not LIV-verified.

## 1. What the report shows

| Fee type | What comparable bodies charge (as reported) |
|---|---|
| Application, non-refundable | NOCN £300; BCS £350 per product group; Highfield £450; Pearson £1,000 (on success); TQUK £1,500; City & Guilds £3,000; HRCI US$100 non-refundable part; NASBA US$1,222 to 6,500; ANAB US$5,000 |
| Annual | Highfield £300 (low spend); BCS £500; HRCI US$525 (1 yr) to 1,425 (3 yr); SHRM US$450 to 800 per 2 yr; NOCN £850; NASBA US$910 to 5,980; TQUK £3,000 (offset by spend); ANAB from US$3,000 |
| Per programme or title review | TQUK £40; Highfield £30; Pearson £135 to 875; NOCN Endorsed £150 to 350; City & Guilds £350 per qualification (£550 for ILM 6/7) |
| Visit or support day | NOCN £375; Highfield £300; UKAS £1,195 per assessor-day plus travel; ANAB US$1,450 per assessor-day |
| Per learner | NOCN e-certificate £7.50; PMI courseware US$39 to 99 |
| Minimum spend | City & Guilds £2,500 a year; Pearson £5,000 a year |
| Speed | CPD Standards Office Fast Track (14 days instead of 30) for an unpublished fee |

Lessons used:
1. **Intake and assessment are separate, and only intake is non-refundable.** The small non-refundable fee filters weak applications and pays the completeness check; the larger fee pays the technical review.
2. **A title or programme review is a small fee; a capability or scope review is a larger one.** Peers charge tens of pounds to a few hundred per title, and more only when the approval itself changes.
3. **Annual fees for comparable provider status sit between roughly £300 and £3,000.** LIV's Starter and Professional tiers are inside that range.
4. **Remote review is the norm for desk-based checks; visits are charged per day.** LIV's remote and on-site day rates are below the assessor-day rates of the accreditation bodies, which assess certification bodies, a heavier task.
5. **Per-learner charges by the awarding body are high because the awarding body issues the credential.** LIV only issues a completion credential, so its per-certificate rate stays low.
6. **Minimum-spend rules and pay-for-speed options exist, and are not suited to LIV** (§5).

## 2. Proposed fee system

### 2.1 Application: intake and assessment (replaces the single combined fee; same totals)

| | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intake fee**, paid at submission, **non-refundable** | $150 | $250 | $400 |
| **Assessment fee**, paid before technical review starts | $350 | $750 | $1,600 (multi-site quoted by scope) |
| Total (unchanged from `FEES.md`) | $500 | $1,000 | $2,000 |

Rules:
- Review work (completeness check onward) starts once the intake fee is received. The fee buys the work, never the decision: an application is decided on the criteria whatever was paid.
- The assessment fee covers the criteria review (C1 to C9), the trainer records of the first three trainers, the first two correction rounds and the decision.
- Assessment fee refund if the applicant withdraws: **100%** before an assessor is assigned; **50%** after assignment and before findings are recorded; **none** once findings are recorded.
- A declined applicant who applies again pays both fees again; there is no credit.
- Each correction round beyond two: **$150**.

### 2.2 Annual authorization (unchanged)

| | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual fee, in advance | $900 | $2,400 | from $6,000 (quoted) |
| Certificates included | 100 | 500 | 2,000 |

Includes: one remote surveillance review a year, the early-operation sample review in the first year, the renewal review, register listing and the partner portal. Renewal is a review decision; paying the invoice does not renew a scope. No minimum-spend rule.

### 2.3 Titles, scope and trainers (new items; replaces the flat "$250 per additional course")

| Item | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Add a title from the LIV catalogue (content already approved) | **$50** | Readiness check of the partner for that title |
| Add a partner's own title inside an authorized scope | **$150** | Short suitability review: description, objectives, audience, duration, delivery, responsible trainer, completion rules |
| Scope expansion (new subdomain, delivery mode, or platform or site capability) | **$450** | One review-day equivalent; covers up to three titles in the new scope |
| Add a trainer after authorization | **$50** each | Document review for criterion C3; the first three trainers are in the assessment fee |
| Change of platform or site | **$150** | Material change notice and review |
| Change of legal name or ownership | $150 | Unchanged |

A title that implies a professional status ("Certified Professional" and similar) is refused by the content review whatever was paid.

### 2.4 Items kept as in `FEES.md`

Special review $450 per remote review day, charged only when the trigger is not LIV's own error; on-site review $750 per day plus travel at cost; reinstatement $500; appeal $300, refunded in full if upheld (complaints are always free); late renewal 10% after 30 days, waived once; certificate issuance $4.00, $3.00, $2.00 graduated after the included allowance; prepaid bundles; correction or re-issue free within 30 days; custom certificate design $300; verification letter $25. Revocation and public verification stay free. The founding-partner, non-profit, multi-year and referral categories are unchanged and apply to the new items as they applied to the old ones (no discount on the intake fee's non-refundable part; discounts never touch the criteria).

### 2.5 Training kit or curriculum pack (later phase; no change proposed now)

The report gives only one comparable (PMI courseware US$39 to 99 per learner for a full licensed programme). LIV's pack licence ($1,200 per curriculum a year plus $6 per learner) has no real comparable until a first LIV curriculum exists; it stays inactive. Set it with the first pack, from actual development cost.

## 4. Why these numbers, and where they are weak

- **Totals are unchanged** from the approved schedule, so the split adds no price rise; only the timing and refund rules change.
- **$150 / $250 / $400 intake** is in line with the non-refundable fees reported (NOCN £300, BCS £350, HRCI $100) scaled to LIV's three tiers.
- **$50 to $150 per title** is inside the range of peer programme-review fees (TQUK £40 to Pearson £135 to NOCN £350); the capability review at **$450** equals LIV's own remote review day.
- **Weakest point:** reviewer time is not measured yet. Re-check after the first five applications: record actual hours per stage and move any fee that is more than about 25% off the cost of the hours.
- Payment and tax: fees are exclusive of taxes; tax treatment (Gulf and MENA digital services) is still for the advisers listed in `FEES.md` §10.

## 5. Considered and not proposed

- **Minimum annual spend** (City & Guilds, Pearson): wrong for small GCC providers and would reward volume over quality.
- **Paid fast track:** it would let payment shorten scrutiny; target turnaround is stated instead, for everyone.
- **Fees tied to outcomes, to ratings or to the use of marks:** forbidden by `FEES.md` §1.
- **Consulting on obtaining authorization:** conflict of interest, unchanged.
- **Per-learner fee to LIV on top of the certificate rate:** duplicates the issuance fee.
