# LIV image brief, part 2: products, library, newsletter, events, partner kit, badges

Date: October 26, 2026. This part follows the same method as `IMAGE-BRIEF.md`: the owner generates the images with an image tool and sends the files; LIV places them. Official records (credentials, scope documents, invoices and legal texts) keep no photographs.

## Rules (same as part 1, with additions)
1. **Style:** editorial, institutional and calm. Natural light, muted palette with deep navy (#0B1F3A) and warm gold (#B08D4C) accents, off-white (#F7F6F2) backgrounds. The images must look like one family with the website images.
2. **People:** adult professionals of mixed ages, genders and backgrounds, in business or safety work wear, with natural poses.
3. **Never in the image:**
   - text or letters,
   - logos,
   - the ISO name or mark,
   - flags or seals,
   - readable documents or screens,
   - the words "accredited" or "certified".
4. **Honesty:** the images are illustrative only, never shown as LIV staff, partners, learners or real events.
5. **Files:** WebP for photos and PNG for the badge and card art. sRGB, the exact size shown, under 300 KB each (badges under 150 KB).
6. **Prompt suffix** for photos: `editorial photography, natural light, muted palette with deep navy and warm gold accents, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermarks, photorealistic`.
7. **Prompt suffix** for graphic items (C1 to C3): `flat vector style, clean geometric shapes, deep navy #0B1F3A and warm gold #B08D4C on transparent or off-white background, no text, no letters, no numbers, crisp edges`.
