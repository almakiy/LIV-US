# Editorial calendar: releasing the library on a steady schedule

Status (October 12, 2026): owner request. The library is not published all at once. Items go live one at a time on a fixed weekly rhythm, grouped into monthly themes. The release-date feature is built: Admin → Knowledge hub content → item → "Release date", then **Publish**.

## Rhythm
- **One item a week, every Tuesday at 12:00 UTC.**
  - That is 8:00 in New York and 15:00 in Riyadh, Dubai and Doha.
  - Tuesday is a working day in both the U.S. and the Gulf.
- **One theme a month.** Each month opens with the main guide on its theme and follows with tools, reference items and a research digest on the same theme. Readers can follow a theme from start to finish.
- **A buffer of reviewed items.** Releases start only when at least four items have passed expert review (K.8). After that, at least four reviewed items are always waiting ahead of the schedule.
  - If review falls behind, the calendar pauses. An item that has not been reviewed is never released to keep a date.
- **Corrections and urgent updates** are published when needed and do not count as a weekly slot. Examples: a law changes, or a regulatory map needs a correction.

## How a release works (the human gate is unchanged)
1. The named reviewer checks the item against its sources using the expert review pack.
2. The editor enters the reviewer's name and the release date from this calendar, then presses **Publish**. The item is approved and shows as **scheduled** in the content list.
3. On the release date the item appears on the Knowledge Hub, in the RSS feed and in the sitemap. Until then it is hidden.
4. The same day, the item is shared on the LIV LinkedIn company page (Track O) and later in a monthly email (Phase 2).

A release date cannot be set in the past, and every scheduled release is recorded in the audit log.

## After the first cycle
- **Weekly releases continue.** New items are added through the same pipeline: written from evidence, checked by the Reviewer, reviewed by an expert, then given a calendar slot.
- **Regulatory content runs on its own clock:**
  - The GCC map and the AI governance item are re-checked every 6 months.
  - A new edition replaces the old one in its slot, with a change note.
- **Each January**, the year's research digests are gathered into the research volume (Track P).
- **The calendar is reviewed every quarter.** Readership figures from the site and LinkedIn can move a theme earlier or later.

## Rules that do not change
- Nothing is released without a named reviewer.
- Nothing is released automatically by an engine.
- A slot is never filled with an unreviewed item.
