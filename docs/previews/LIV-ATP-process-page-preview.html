LIV Authorized Training Provider (ATP)

Your Path to Becoming a LIV Authorized Training Provider

Apply for authorization within a defined training scope. LIV reviews your organization's delivery capability, trainers, training methods and supporting evidence before making an authorization decision.

What LIV ATP Authorization Covers

LIV ATP authorization permits a provider to deliver training within a scope approved by LIV. Course titles and versions must be approved before they can be used for credential requests.

The current program supports Training Completion Credentials only. Providers deliver training and submit completion records; LIV issues the credentials after the required checks. These credentials do not require an examination, do not expire, and are not professional qualifications or professional certifications.

Authorization is scope-specific. Payment does not guarantee approval or activate issuance rights.

The process

  1. 1
    Explore & Request a Quote

    Understand eligibility, scope, services and fees.

  2. 2
    Apply & Submit Evidence

    Your organization profile, the field you request, your trainers, delivery methods and supporting evidence.

  3. 3
    Completeness Check

    LIV checks that the file is complete and the initial eligibility conditions are met. If something is missing, LIV asks you to supply it.

  4. 4
    Technical Review

    LIV reviews your actual capability and evidence, and may request an interview or a sample where the policy and risk call for it.

  5. 5
    Independent Decision

    Acceptance to activation, acceptance with recorded conditions, or a reasoned refusal. The reviewer does not make the decision on the same application.

  6. 6
    Activate Your Authorized Scope

    Required conditions and blocking items are cleared before your scope is switched on and listed in the register.

  7. 7
    Maintain Your Authorization

    Monitoring, notice of material changes, and re-assessment and renewal under the published policy.

If the file needs work or is refused

Information / Corrective Action

You submit new evidence. The application returns to the review stage it needs. Nothing is approved automatically.

Declined

You receive the reasons and the appeal route set out in the published policy. A declined application does not move to activation, and an appeal does not grant issuance rights.

From Approved Training to a LIV Credential

Approved scope → Approved course title and version → Training delivery record → Completion evidence → LIV issuance checks → Verifiable credential.

Unchanged approved courses do not require a new content review for every delivery. Delivery records, reporting requirements and material changes are governed by the applicable LIV policy.

All application and credential submission data must be provided in English. Credentials are issued in English only.

What happens if the application is incomplete?

If information is missing or a requirement is not met, LIV may request clarification or corrective action. Acceptance, activation and ongoing authorization are separate stages. Decisions, conditions and next steps are recorded and communicated to the applicant.
