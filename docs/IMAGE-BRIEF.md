# LIV website image brief

Date: October 26, 2026. The owner generates these images with an image tool, then sends the files to be placed. Each entry gives the page and position, the file name, the size, a short description, a ready prompt, and the alt text.

## Rules for every image
1. **Style:** editorial, institutional and calm. Natural daylight, soft shadows, shallow depth of field. Muted colors with deep navy (#0B1F3A) and warm gold (#B08D4C) accents. Off-white backgrounds (#F7F6F2). No neon, no heavy gradients, no "tech glow".
2. **People:** adult professionals of mixed ages, genders and backgrounds. Modest, business or safety work wear. Natural poses, no posing at the camera, no exaggerated smiles.
3. **Never in the image:**
   - text or letters,
   - logos (LIV or any other),
   - the ISO name or mark,
   - flags or government seals,
   - readable certificates, documents or screens,
   - stamps or the words "accredited" or "certified".
4. **Honesty:** generated images are illustrative only. They must never be captioned or presented as LIV staff, real partners, real learners or real events. Real photos of the team and partners come later, with written consent.
5. **Files:** WebP or JPG, sRGB, at least the size shown, under 300 KB after compression. Hero images need their main subject on the right half, because text sits on the left.
6. **Prompt suffix** to add to every prompt: `editorial photography, natural light, muted palette with deep navy and warm gold accents, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermarks, photorealistic`.
